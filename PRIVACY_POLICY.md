# NRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

NRM collects the phone number used to communicate with the service, message content, and any contact or interaction details that a user sends to NRM.

We do not share, sell, or provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent data to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

The only exception is the infrastructure providers necessarily involved in sending and receiving these messages (Twilio, Cloudflare, and Google), who process data solely to operate NRM and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

Message frequency varies based on user activity.

Message and data rates may apply.
