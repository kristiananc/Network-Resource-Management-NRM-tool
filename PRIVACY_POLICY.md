# NRM Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

NRM collects the phone number used to communicate with the service, message content, and any contact or interaction details that a user sends to NRM.

This information is used solely to operate NRM as a personal relationship-management tool for the account owner and a small group of invited testers. It is not sold, used for marketing, or shared with third parties for their own purposes. NRM does not share mobile information or messaging consent with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes.

Mobile phone numbers are not shared, sold, or disclosed to third parties or affiliates for any purpose, except as required to operate NRM itself (e.g. the SMS/MMS infrastructure providers — Twilio, Cloudflare, Google — that are necessarily part of sending and receiving these messages).

Service providers may process data only as necessary to deliver and operate NRM's messaging and infrastructure. They are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.
